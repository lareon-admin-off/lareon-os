# Lareon brand kit

For Lareon OS (and anything else). Use these exact files; do not redraw the logos.

## Logos (in `logos/`)
- `lareon-icon.svg` is the Lareon company mark: one symmetrical bird-like shape with a pointed head, wings swept out to each side and a split tail, filled with a gradient from orange at the bottom to violet at the top. PNGs at 256, 512 and 1024 with transparent backgrounds. `lareon-app-icon.png` is the icon used by the Lareon desktop app.
- Each product has its own mark. Do not mix them up:
  - `fledge-logo.svg` (mail): a fan of swept feather blades, orange and violet.
  - `aster-logo.svg` (assistant): a five-petal fan of gold-to-coral blades, like a small lotus.
  - `dusk-logo.svg` (calendar, tasks and notes): five overlapping arches in a fan, violet to orange.
  - `ember-logo.svg` (browser): two overlapping teardrops, one magenta and one orange.

## Colors
- Brand gradient: orange `#ff7c1f` to magenta `#d94fc4` (at 60%) to violet `#a35cf5`.
- Dark theme: background `#13111b`, panel `#1a1824`, panel 2 `#211e2d`, lines `#2b2838`, text `#ece9f5`, muted `#a7a4ba`, faint `#74718a`, accent orange `#ff7c1f`, violet `#a35cf5`.
- Light theme: background `#f4effb`, text `#1d1832`, muted `#4f4a68`.

## Type
- Lareon's websites use **Geist** (and **Geist Mono** for small labels). They are free and open (SIL OFL). Download them from https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist and https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist+Mono and add the files in `fonts/`. The desktop app itself uses the system font.

## Product names (spell exactly)
Lareon, Fledge, Aster, Dusk, Ember. One free Lareon account works across all of them.
